A system is something that reproduces itself. Everything I create is meant to become its own system, outliving me and continuing to inspire, transform, and grow.

These efforts are rooted in two principles: representation is constitutive and meaning is constructed.

Representation is Constitutive

Representation shapes the fabric of reality itself. It forms the structures through which we understand ourselves and the world.

Meaning is Constructed

Meaning emerges through relationships, culture, and context. It is an ongoing process of creation that transforms how we interpret and interact with the world.

Daniel Twafe, Three Faces of Africa
