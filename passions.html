Ethiopian Public Debate Program

Through a Fulbright Fellowship, I am establishing Ethiopia's first Public Debate Program. Students from five cities compete in city and national championships, culminating in the formation of Ethiopia's first National Debate Team, which will represent the country at the World Schools Debating Championships.
